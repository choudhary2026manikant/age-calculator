Free Online Age Calculator

Our free Age Calculator helps you calculate your exact age from your date of birth. Instead of simply showing your age in years, it calculates the complete difference in years, months and days.

You can also choose a specific date to find out how old you were or will be on that date. The calculator automatically calculates your next birthday and the remaining number of days.

How to Calculate Your Age

Enter your date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format. The calculator uses the selected Age as on Date to calculate your exact age.

Age Calculator for India

This calculator supports the commonly used DD/MM/YYYY date format in India and is designed to make age calculation simple and convenient.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Age Calculator work?

Enter your date of birth and the date on which you want to calculate your age. The calculator then displays your exact age in years, months and days.

Can I calculate my age on a specific date?

Yes. Enter any valid Age as on Date after your date of birth. The calculator will calculate your exact age on that date.

Can I find my next birthday?

Yes. After calculating your age, the calculator automatically displays your next birthday and the number of days remaining.

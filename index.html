What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is an online tool that calculates a person's age from their date of birth to a selected date.

This calculator can show your exact age in complete years, months and days. You can also calculate your age as on a specific date.

What Does "Age as on Date" Mean?

Age as on date means calculating how old you were on a particular date instead of calculating only your current age.

For example, you can enter your date of birth and choose a specific date to find your exact age on that date.

This can be useful when checking age for forms, applications, eligibility dates, records and other situations where an age needs to be calculated on a particular date.

How to Calculate Your Age

To calculate your exact age, enter your date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format and choose the date on which you want to calculate your age.

  1. Enter your date of birth.
  2. Enter the Age as on Date.
  3. Click the Calculate Age button.
  4. Your exact age will be displayed in years, months and days.
  5. You can also see your next birthday and approximate life statistics.

How Does This Age Calculator Work?

The calculator compares your date of birth with the selected calculation date.

The difference is divided into complete years, months and remaining days.

The calculator also determines your next birthday relative to the selected date.

Life statistics such as total days, weeks, hours, minutes and seconds are provided as approximate values based on the calculated number of days.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this age calculator?

What date format should I use?

What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is an online tool that calculates a person's age from their date of birth to the current date.

Instead of manually counting years, months and days, an age calculator provides the result quickly and accurately.

How to Calculate Your Age

To calculate your exact age, simply enter your date of birth in the date field above and click the Calculate Age button.

  1. Select your date of birth.
  2. Click the Calculate Age button.
  3. Your exact age will be displayed in years, months and days.
  4. You can also see your next birthday and approximate life statistics.

How Does This Age Calculator Work?

This calculator compares your date of birth with today's date and calculates the difference between them.

The result is divided into complete years, months and remaining days. The calculator also determines the date of your next birthday.

Life statistics such as total days, weeks, hours, minutes and seconds are provided as approximate values based on the calculated number of days.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this age calculator?

The calculator uses your date of birth and the current date to calculate your age in complete years, months and days.

Can I calculate my age in days?

Yes. The Life Statistics section shows the approximate total number of days you have lived.

Can I find my next birthday?

Yes. The calculator automatically shows your next birthday and the number of days remaining until it.

Is this age calculator free?

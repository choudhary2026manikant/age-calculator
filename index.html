Age Calculator India

This free Age Calculator India helps you calculate your exact age from your date of birth. Enter your date of birth in the Indian DD/MM/YYYY format to find your age in complete years, months and days.

You can also choose a specific "Age as on Date" to calculate how old you were on a particular date. The calculator works directly in your browser and does not require you to create an account.

Age Calculator by Date of Birth

An age calculator by date of birth determines the difference between your birth date and a selected calculation date. Instead of showing only the number of years, this calculator provides the exact difference in years, months and days.

For example, if your date of birth is 15/08/1995, you can enter that date and calculate your exact age as of today or as of any other date you choose.

Age Calculator in DD/MM/YYYY Format

The calculator uses the DD/MM/YYYY date format commonly used in India. For example, 25 December 1990 should be entered as 25/12/1990.

The date fields automatically format your entry with slashes, making it easier to enter an Indian-style date correctly.

Age as on Date Calculator

"Age as on Date" means calculating your age on a particular date rather than only on today's date.

This can be useful when checking age for forms, applications, eligibility dates, records, admission-related information and other situations where an age needs to be calculated for a particular date.

How to Calculate Your Age

  1. Enter your Date of Birth in DD/MM/YYYY format.
  2. Enter the Age as on Date.
  3. Click the Calculate Age button.
  4. Your exact age will be displayed in years, months and days.
  5. You can also see your next birthday, days until your birthday and life statistics.

What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is an online utility that calculates a person's age from their date of birth to a selected date.

A simple year-only calculation may not show the complete difference between two dates. An exact age calculator can break the result into complete years, months and remaining days.

How Does This Age Calculator Work?

The calculator compares your date of birth with the selected calculation date.

The difference is calculated as complete years, complete months and remaining days.

The calculator also determines the next birthday relative to the selected date.

Life statistics such as total days, weeks, hours, minutes and seconds are approximate calculations based on the total number of completed days between the two dates.

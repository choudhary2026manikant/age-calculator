ABOUT

A smarter way to calculate age.

Our free Age Calculator makes it easy to calculate your exact age from your date of birth. Instead of giving you only a number of years, it shows the complete difference in years, months and days.

You can also choose a specific date to calculate your age on that date. The tool automatically calculates your next birthday, remaining days and detailed life statistics.

How does the Age Calculator work?

Enter your date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format, choose the date you want to calculate your age on, and press the calculate button. The result is calculated instantly in your browser.

What Is an Age Calculator in India?

An age calculator is an online tool that calculates the difference between a person's date of birth and a selected date.

In India, dates are commonly written in DD/MM/YYYY format. For example, 15 August 1995 can be written as 15/08/1995.

An exact age calculator can show the result in complete years, months and days instead of showing only the number of years.

What Does Age as on Date Mean?

"Age as on Date" means finding a person's age on a particular date rather than only calculating their current age.

For example, someone may want to know their exact age on 01/01/2027 even though the calculation is being performed on a different date.

This type of calculation can be useful when a form, application or record asks for age on a particular date.

DD/MM/YYYY Date Format in India

The DD/MM/YYYY format places the day first, followed by the month and then the year.

Examples:

  1. 5 January 2000 → 05/01/2000
  2. 15 August 1995 → 15/08/1995
  3. 26 January 1990 → 26/01/1990

When entering a date, make sure that the day and month are valid. For example, 31/02/2000 is not a valid calendar date.
